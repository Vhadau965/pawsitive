About Pawsitive Pet Academy

Learn more about our history, vision, mission and goals.

Our History

Pawsitive Pet Academy was established in Durban in 2023 by Sarah Smith.

The academy was created to provide practical and accessible education in pet care and animal training.

Hundreds of pet owners and aspiring pet-care professionals have completed our programmes and short courses.

Our Vision

To become a trusted provider of quality pet-care and animal-training education in South Africa.

Our Mission

To provide practical, affordable and professional education that promotes responsible pet ownership and animal wellbeing.

Our Goals

  • Improve knowledge of responsible pet ownership.
  • Promote animal health and wellbeing.
  • Provide practical animal-training skills.
  • Support people interested in pet-care careers.
  • Encourage employment and small-business opportunities.
